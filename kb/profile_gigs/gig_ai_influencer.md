# Gig: I will design a realistic AI influencer for your brand or Instagram

## Tagline
Get Your Own Realistic AI Influencer Made For Your Brand

## Seller pitch
Full-time AI content creator with 2+ years of experience building realistic AI models, virtual influencers, and faceless UGC content for brands, agencies, and personal pages. Builds consistent AI characters for Instagram, product ads, or faceless content pages that look real, not the plastic, uncanny-valley kind.

## What's delivered
- A realistic, consistent AI model — same face, every image
- Custom look: body type, style, ethnicity, outfit, vibe, all client's call
- High-resolution images, sharp and social-media ready
- Short-form video/reels using the AI model (higher packages)
- Commercial usage rights — post it, sell it, run ads with it

## How it works
1. Client sends a brief: style, niche, references if available
2. Seller builds and fine-tunes the AI character
3. Client reviews the first set and approves the look
4. Seller delivers the full package: images, reels, and source files

## Good for
Instagram/TikTok faceless pages, product & ad creatives, virtual brand ambassadors, personal branding for people who don't want to be on camera, agencies producing content at scale.
