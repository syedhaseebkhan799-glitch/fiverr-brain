*This is a placeholder summary in plain language. Before relying on this for real business decisions, verify the exact current wording on Fiverr's official Terms of Service and Community Standards pages.*

## Core expectations for sellers
- Deliver what was promised in the gig description, on time, at the agreed package scope
- Communicate through Fiverr's official messaging system — avoid taking payment or contact details off-platform, which risks account penalties
- Do not misrepresent gig deliverables (e.g., claiming "commercial usage rights" if that isn't actually offered)
- Handle buyer disputes through Fiverr's resolution/support process rather than off-platform pressure

## Revisions
Only the number of revisions stated in the gig package should be treated as included; anything beyond that is typically a paid extra unless the seller chooses to offer it as goodwill.

## Cancellations
Frequent order cancellations can affect seller ranking/level, so cancellations should be a last resort, used only when a job genuinely cannot be completed as scoped.
