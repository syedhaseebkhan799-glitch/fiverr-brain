# Policy Summary: Fiverr Seller Fees

*Summarized in own words from third-party seller-fee breakdowns, current as of 2026. Always verify against Fiverr's own Help Center / Terms of Service before treating as final.*

## Seller commission
Fiverr deducts a flat **20% service fee** from every completed order — no tiers, no volume discounts, and no reduction based on seller level or history. This applies to the full order value, including gig extras and buyer tips.

Example: a $100 order credits the seller $80; Fiverr keeps $20.

## Buyer-side fees (context, not charged to seller)
Buyers separately pay a checkout service fee (commonly cited around 5.5%) plus a small fixed fee on smaller orders (frequently cited as ~$2.50 on orders under $50). These do not reduce seller earnings directly but do affect what buyers see at checkout — worth knowing when a buyer questions the total price.

## Withdrawal costs
Getting funds out of Fiverr to a bank/payment method incurs its own separate withdrawal fee, which varies by method (e.g., direct deposit vs. PayPal vs. other payout providers). Currency conversion may add further cost for non-USD sellers.

## Practical takeaway for pricing
Since 20% is fixed, if a target take-home amount is known, divide by 0.80 to get the gig price needed to net that amount after Fiverr's cut.

## Source note
This summary is compiled from multiple third-party seller-fee guides (not Fiverr's own site directly) and should be re-verified periodically against Fiverr's official Help Center, since fee structures can change.
